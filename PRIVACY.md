# WellShare privacy notice

Share this with participants before they install the app. Edit the contact line for your hostel.

**Who runs it:** your hostel's mentoring programme, on a database the programme controls (Supabase).

**What the app sends, once every few hours:**

- Total screen time per day
- Number of phone unlocks per day
- Screen time between 00:00 and 05:00
- Time spent in your top 20 apps, with each app's name
- Your name, roll number, phone model and Android version
- A one-way scrambled code (SHA-256 hash) made from your phone's hardware ID. It lets the system keep one phone linked to one person. The real hardware ID never leaves your phone.

**What it never collects:** messages, photos, contacts, call logs, what you type, watch or browse inside apps, passwords, or location.

**Who can see it:** only the mentors of your group, after signing in. Other students can't see your data. The phone app can send data but can't read anyone's data back.

**Your choices:**

- Taking part should be voluntary. You see the same numbers in the app that your mentor sees.
- Tap "Stop sharing on this phone" in the app, or uninstall it, to stop sending.
- Ask your mentor to "Reset phone link" to delete everything stored about you.

**How long it's kept:** until your mentor resets your link or the programme deletes its database.
