Group settings: SOS number and group app timers

SOS phone number

The SOS button on students' phones calls this number and sends you an alert with their location. Use a phone that's always with a mentor or warden.

App timers for the whole group

A daily limit for every student in this group. When it runs out, the app closes on their phone until midnight. A timer set on one student's page replaces the group timer for that student. Phones pick up changes within about an hour. Calls, messages and Settings can't be limited.

    min/day

    Screen time is the time apps were open on screen, counted the same way as Android's Digital Wellbeing. After midnight means 00:00–05:00. Phones report about every hour. Use these numbers to start a conversation, not to judge one day.
